Built for landlords

Rented properties normally need an EICR at least every five years. Gold keeps that report, the photos and the next due date on a page linked from the board itself.

QR on the board

Scan the sticker on the consumer unit to open that property's certificates, photos and history. No searching through old emails.

One view of every rental

Landlords with more than one property get an overview page. A button turns red when an EICR has expired.

Insurance and safety

A current EICR helps show the installation has been inspected. An expired report can cause problems if there is a claim after an electrical incident.
