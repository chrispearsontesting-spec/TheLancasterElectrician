Security & backup

Your cars, photos and invoices live only on this phone. Lock the app, and keep a backup file somewhere safe.

App lock Off

Ask for a PIN when Driv3 opens, and again after 5 minutes in the background. This is a privacy screen, so someone picking up your phone can't browse your cars. It doesn't encrypt the data stored on the phone.

Forgot the PIN? The only way out is clearing Driv3's data in your browser, which deletes your cars on this phone. Keep a backup so you can restore them.

Make a backup

You haven't made a backup on this phone yet.

One locked file with everything: cars, reminders, history, photos, PDFs, mechanic links and shared reports. Save it to Google Drive or email it to yourself.

Write the passphrase down. If you lose it, nobody can open the backup, not even us.

Restore a backup

Use this on a new phone, or after clearing Driv3's data.

This replaces everything Driv3 has on this phone with what's in the backup. Anything added since the backup was made will be lost. Your app lock PIN stays as it is.
