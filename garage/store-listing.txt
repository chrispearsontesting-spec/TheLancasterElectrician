Full description
Dipstick keeps a car in one place. Reminders for MOT, tax, insurance, service, timing belt and tyres. Route costs from today's UK fuel price. Service estimates for a local garage, a main dealer and DIY. Buying guide from the registration, with MOT history. Optional dashcam and drive diary on the phone.

No account. Dates and documents stay on the device.
