How the Gold EICR works

For landlords, agents and homeowners

An EICR is an Electrical Installation Condition Report. It is the formal inspection and test of the fixed wiring in a property, carried out to BS 7671. For rented homes in England it is normally required at least every five years, and when a new tenancy starts if the last report is out of date.

Why this matters. An expired or missing EICR can cause problems if there is a claim after an electrical fire or injury. Insurers can reduce or refuse a claim where the installation was not properly maintained or certified. A current report is the simple way to show you have taken safety seriously.

What you get with Gold

The test itself is the same professional EICR as the Standard package. Gold adds a digital record that stays with the property.

  • Clear next-due date, with a red warning when a test has expired
  • Troubleshooting guide from the same QR code
  • For landlords with more than one property: one overview page with a button per rental

How it works in practice

1. Chris carries out the EICR at the property.
2. A QR sticker goes on the distribution board.
